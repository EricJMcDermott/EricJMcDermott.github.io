THE CONTEXT

The problem to work on.

An AI capability becomes useful when it fits the work people actually need to do. At diconium, my remit connects user needs and business decisions with the systems, evaluation, and rollout required to put AI into production.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Where I took responsibility.

  1. Build the team and the product practice.

    Created and lead a seven-person AI R&D team spanning data science, business analysis, UX, and product strategy. My responsibility covers discovery through architecture, implementation, guardrails, rollout, and adoption.

  2. Stay hands-on across the system.

    Develop full-stack AI products across LLM and machine-learning pipelines, APIs, backend services, interfaces, databases, and cloud infrastructure. Work includes enterprise knowledge assistants, multimodal knowledge platforms, and natural-language analytics.

  3. Connect technical work to business decisions.

    Work on AI roadmaps and maturity models alongside product delivery. This includes a Volkswagen AI roadmap and a proof of concept connected to a data-platform opportunity.
